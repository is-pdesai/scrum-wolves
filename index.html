Why wolves?

Wolves are the world's largest wild canids — apex predators that hunt in coordinated packs, communicate across miles of forest, and raise pups cooperatively. They are also the ancestors of every dog you have ever loved. This site is our team's tribute to them.

Use the navigation above to explore eight aspects of wolf life: where they live, what they eat, how they raise their young, what their bodies do that ours can't, and how scientists classify them. Every page links back here.
