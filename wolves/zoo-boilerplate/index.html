🏛️ About Us

The story of WildNation Zoo & Wildlife Centre

A World of Wildlife, Right Here

WildNation Zoo & Wildlife Centre was founded in 1985 with a simple but powerful mission: to bring the wild world to people — and inspire the next generation to protect it.

Nestled in the heart of the countryside, our award-winning centre is home to over 200 animals across 50 species. From the lightning strike of a cobra to the haunting howl of a wolf pack, WildNation Zoo brings wildlife education to life in ways you'll never forget.

We believe that understanding wildlife leads to protecting it. Every exhibit, every show, and every educational programme is designed to deepen your connection with the natural world.

🌿 Our Mission

To inspire the next generation of wildlife champions through education, conservation, and unforgettable hands-on wildlife experiences.
